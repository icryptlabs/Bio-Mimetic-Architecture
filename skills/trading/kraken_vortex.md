# Kraken Vortex Protocol
1. **Autonomous Execution:** Utilize the **Kraken CLI** (zero-dependency Rust binary) to retrieve market data and execute programmatic trades [Source 905, 903].
2. **Verifiable Trading:** Link every Kraken trade to the agent's **ERC-8004** identity. Post 'validation artifacts' to the ledger to prove performance transparently [Source 947].
3. **Risk Gating:** Submit **TradeIntents** through the whitelisted Risk Router to enforce position size limits and stop-loss policies on-chain [Source 902, 948].
4. **Social Engagement:** Automate the agent to post its trade rationale and development progress publicly to satisfy the Social Engagement mandate [Source 949, 908].
