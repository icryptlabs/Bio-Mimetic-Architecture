# Mycelial Mesh Protocol
1. **LoRa Signaling:** Utilize LoRa radios for long-range signaling without a 'Cloud Bloodstream' or router [Source 756, 757].
2. **Encrypted Telepathy:** Maintain agent-to-agent communication via encrypted mesh packets to prevent surveillance [Source 756, 757].
3. **Resilience:** If the internet connection is lost, transition to mesh-only signaling to remain grounded and functional [Source 1003].
